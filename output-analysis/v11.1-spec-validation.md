## Gaps (3)

### G-1 — What the cap of a pause window bounds

- **Spec section:** §4.5.4 (L939), PLY-24 (L941), §5.1.4 `@durationCap` (L2016), §8.13 item 8 (L3546).
- **What the context is missing:** `context/` requires the cap on every pause slot (R4.1, R4.10) and says it bounds nothing there: R4 prose — *"A **pause** slot has no declared duration for a cap to bound"* (`../context/03-requirements.md` L381-382) — and R31.2 — *"on a pause slot it bounds neither"* (L498). R4.2 at the same time lists *"the non-linear families"* among those where the cap bounds cumulative duration (L398). UC-05 states *"No maximum display duration"* (`../context/04-use-cases.md` L591). The context therefore requires a value and gives it no meaning.
- **What an implementer does today:** follows the spec's own answer, *"The cumulative duration of **one pass** through the candidates of one resolution document"* (L939), which is an invention the spec itself lists as open (L3546). Proprietary in effect: two implementers reading only `context/` could give the cap two meanings.
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-1).

### G-2 — The spec's open questions are not recorded as open in `context/`

- **Spec section:** §8.13 (L3503-3559), nine numbered questions.
- **What the context is missing:** the "Deliberately open" table of `../context/03-requirements.md` (L2299-2300) is empty, and the text under it says so is a statement that *"no silence in this specification has been declared deliberate yet"*. Nine questions the spec had to answer to be implementable (Advanced Linear profile, supersede timing, on-top cap timeline, live pause beyond the time-shift buffer, skip-control precedence, pause window in an alternative presentation, PLY-55 applicability, the pause cap, the empty List MPD under the List profile) are therefore silences nobody chose.
- **What an implementer does today:** takes the spec's provisional answers as settled; nothing marks them as revisitable except §8.13 itself.
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-2).

### G-3 — The standalone non-linear resolution document is not among R1.2's extension points

- **Spec section:** §4.7.6 C4 (L1676-1697), APS-11 (L770-774).
- **What the context is missing:** R1.2 enumerates three admissible extension points — foreign-namespace open content, application-level Event Streams, descriptor schemes. `<svta:OverlayList>` is none of them: the spec says *"A standalone XML document, root element `<svta:OverlayList>` … It is not an MPD and is not embedded in one"* (L1678-1680), and gives as its extension point *"It is reached only through a window's `@uri`, which only a Player of this specification resolves"* (L1681-1682). APS-11 then calls the non-AV asset carrier inside it *"foreign-namespace content (§5.3), which is one of the carriers §4.7.1 enumerates"* (L773-774), although inside an SVTA-rooted document the SVTA elements are not foreign content in the DASH §5.2.1 sense. `context/08-dash-extension-rules.md` discusses the document (DR-10) without admitting it to R1.2's list.
- **What an implementer does today:** accepts the spec's argument; a strict reading of R1.2 and R24.1 would reject the whole non-linear path.
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-3). Coverage rows R1.2, R24#p2, R24.1 are `partial` for this reason.

## Edge cases (4)

### EC-1 — Which timeline runs while a pause ad is on screen

- **Trigger:** a pause ad is presented while the primary presentation timeline is frozen (PLY-62), and the slot carries a dismissal delay, tracking beacons, or a `repeat` behaviour bounded per pass by the cap.
- **Why it matters:** §5.2.4 measures the dismissal delay *"on the presentation timeline of the slot, like the cap (PLY-31)"* (L2180-2181); §5.5.2 says beacon times *"stop accruing while the presentation timeline does not advance"* (L2437-2438); PLY-31 says an interval where *"the presentation timeline does not advance MUST NOT accrue against the cap"* (L971-972). Read against the primary timeline, a pause ad never becomes dismissible, never fires a beacon after time 0, and a `repeat` pass never ends by the cap. Annex E (E.6-E.9) and R-PLY-17 (L7040) assume the pause ad's own timeline advances. The spec does not say which timeline "the presentation timeline" is for a pause slot.
- **Responsible actor:** Player. `context/` is silent (R4.11 speaks of suspended overlays, R35.2 of "the moment the slot begins rendering", R13 of the ad's presentation time).
- **Routing:** 5.b (F-1).

### EC-2 — Dismissing a pause slot under `request-again` or `repeat`

- **Trigger:** the viewer dismisses a pause ad while still paused, on a document declaring `request-again` or `repeat`.
- **Why it matters:** PLY-75 ends *"the **whole slot**"* and forbids advancing *"to another ad or another form within it"* (L1231-1233), which stops `repeat`. Whether `request-again` may still issue a new request in the same pause, whose document is arguably a new slot, is not stated; §4.5.11 leaves open whether a second request is the same opportunity (L1188-1193). A Player that re-requests after a dismissal puts back the surface the viewer just dismissed.
- **Responsible actor:** Player. `context/` is silent (R35 and R32 do not meet).
- **Routing:** 5.b (F-2).

### EC-3 — An explicit `@allowedLayouts` carrying tokens of another family

- **Trigger:** an overlay window declares `allowedLayouts="pause-partial"`, or a pause window declares `linear` or `overlay-lower-third`.
- **Why it matters:** PUB-4 admits any token of §3.4.2 (L636-638) and the schema types `@allowedLayouts` as the full `LayoutTokenListType` (L2755, L2769). The family restriction exists only for the default (§3.4.3). The composition of a pause token over playing content, or of a `linear` takeover while the viewer is paused, is not defined anywhere (§5.3.6 L2324 describes `linear` only against static and dynamic playback). §8.2 E8 mentions only *"of another family on a window that declares none"* (L3389).
- **Responsible actor:** Publisher (what it may declare) and Player (what it does with it). `context/` is silent: R38.1 restricts only the undeclared case, R12.2 only to the token list.
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-14).

### EC-4 — A legacy Player without DASH Annex I.3 meets an SGAI `RequestParam`

- **Trigger:** the Publisher attaches the optional `RequestParam` with the SGAI request-type URN (§5.8.1), which brings the MPD-level `EssentialProperty` of scheme `urn:mpeg:dash:urlparam:2025`, and a base Player that does not implement Annex I.3 loads the MPD.
- **Why it matters:** the spec states the outcome itself: *"at MPD level that parent is the MPD, so such a Player does not play the primary content"* (L1720-1721), and assigns the cost to the base (*"The cost is the base's, and the Publisher takes it on by attaching `RequestParam`, which is optional"*, L1724-1725). R1 requires the opposite for every construct this specification introduces.
- **Responsible actor:** Publisher (authoring) and the spec document (placement). `context/` is not silent: R1.1 and R1's prose forbid it. See coverage rows R1#p2, R1.1 (`contradicted`), DP-3#p1 and UC-07 (`partial`).
- **Routing:** 5.b (F-3).

## Ambiguities (15)

### A-1 — Which pause windows apply while a linear ad is on screen

- **Context passage:** R17.5 (`../context/03-requirements.md` L1567-1574) — any pause *"inside a pause opportunity window while a linear ad occupies the screen"* presents the pause ad; R40.4 (L1830-1835) — a window with no relation presents *"only while the content of the presentation whose `MPD` declares the window is being output"*.
- **Readings:** (a) every pause window whose span contains the pause applies during a linear ad (R17.5 literally); (b) only windows of the linear ad's own MPD, or `on-top` windows of the triggering presentation, apply (R40.4 and R40.5).
- **Spec assumed:** (b) — PLY-55: *"A pause window is applicable to a linear ad when it is declared in that ad's own MPD (PLY-51) or when it is a window of the triggering presentation that declares `on-top`"* (L1128-1131), recorded as open at L3543-3545. It is the reading consistent with R40.
- **Tighter sentence:** R17.5 — "When a viewer pause begins, while a linear ad occupies the screen, inside a pause opportunity window that applies to that ad under R40 (declared in the ad's own `MPD`, or declaring on top), the Player MUST …".
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-4); coverage row R17.5 `partial`.

### A-2 — Supersede on a pause window

- **Context passage:** R40.1 (L1815-1817) — *"A window declares at most one relation, supersede or on top"*, on any non-linear window.
- **Readings:** (a) a pause window may declare supersede; (b) it may not, because whether it presents an ad is unknown at the linear event's presentation time.
- **Spec assumed:** (b) — *"On a **pause window**, `@linearRelation` takes only the value `on-top`"* (L2045), enforced in the schema by `OnTopOnlyType` (L2721-2725, L2771).
- **Tighter sentence:** R40.1 — "An overlay window declares at most one relation, supersede or on top; a pause window may declare only on top."
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-5); coverage row R40.1 `partial`.

### A-3 — "Composition attribute of the slot / layout" for the double-box background

- **Context passage:** R26.1 (L1390-1392) — *"MUST be carried as a composition attribute of the slot / layout, not as a separate presentation option (R5)"*.
- **Readings:** (a) an attribute of the slot declaration in the MPD; (b) an attribute of the layout as the resolution document carries it, which is on the option that names the layout.
- **Spec assumed:** (b) — `@background` on `<svta:RenderableAsset>`, *"Present if and only if `@layout="squeezeback-double-box-background"`"* (L2263), described as *"a composition attribute of the option"* (APS-13, L780-782). (a) is not available: the background is the advertiser's creative and the slot is the Publisher's, and R26.1 names the APS. The prohibited form — a separate option — is not used.
- **Tighter sentence:** R26.1 — "The background element MUST be carried with the presentation option whose layout is the double box, as an attribute of that layout and not as a separate presentation option (R5)."
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-9); coverage row R26.1 `met` under reading (b).

### A-4 — Whether DP-1.2's base-pair carve-out reaches `<svta:Ad>@duration`

- **Context passage:** DP-1.2 (L37-48) — the carve-out covers *"Two declarations the base specification itself provides, and reconciles by its own rule"*.
- **Readings:** (a) the carve-out covers only base pairs (Linked Period `@duration` / imported `Period@duration`), so `<svta:Ad>@duration` plus the video sub-MPD's `Period@duration` is a duplication; (b) a pair of which one member is this spec's and which is reconciled by the base's rule is covered by analogy.
- **Spec assumed:** (b) — *"for a video option, the Player reconciles it with the sub-MPD's `Period@duration` by keeping the smaller of the two, as the base does for a Linked Period"* (L2244), while DOC-6 says every other value *"has exactly one canonical declaration, and the others are derived from it at runtime"* (L1348-1350).
- **Tighter sentence:** either DP-1.2 adds "or one this specification declares next to a base declaration it cannot remove, reconciled by the base rule", or the spec derives the candidate's duration from the sub-MPD for video-only candidates.
- **Routing:** 5.b (F-4); coverage row DP-1.2#p1 `contradicted` under reading (a), which is the literal one.

## Per-category notes

- **Conforming (audit) — not comparable, marked `n/c`.** The two audits
  did not partition the same construct set: v7 audited **40 rows**
  (C1–C26 + M1–M9 + NC1–NC5), v7.1 audited **30 rows** (C1–C30, with
  the Marginal / Non-conforming verdicts carried inline). The partition
  itself differs — v7's row `C7` bundles nine SGAI elements
  (`<svta:Candidate>`, `<svta:RenderableAsset>`, `<svta:BackgroundElement>`,
  `<svta:Click>`, `<svta:ClickTracking>`, `<svta:AdSystem>`,
  `<svta:AdTitle>`, `<svta:Advertiser>`, `<svta:UniversalAdId>`) into one
  verdict, where v7.1 splits the same elements across C9, C10, C11, C12
  and C14. The absolute drop 26 → 21 is therefore a denominator artefact,
  not a conformance regression: the conforming **rate** rose from 26/40
  (65%) to 21/30 (70%). The issue categories that feed the Total
  (Marginal, Non-conforming) count distinct findings in both audits
  (v7: M1–M9, NC1–NC5; v7.1: M1–M6, NC1–NC2) and are comparable.
- **R coverage governance -3 and partial +2 are one reclassification,
  not a regression.** Requirement-by-requirement, five rows changed
  status between the two independent validators: R11, R18 and R23 moved
  `governance` → `full`, and R4 and R12 moved `full` → `partial`. No row
  moved into `gap`, and no requirement lost coverage. The +2 on `partial`
  is charged to the Total as the contract requires; the -3 on
  `governance` prints ⚠️ under the contract's rule for a "good" category,
  but it is offset by the matching +1 on `full` and is not a loss of
  governance argumentation.

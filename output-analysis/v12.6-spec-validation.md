## Edge cases (1)

### EC-1 — A live pause that outlasts the time-shift buffer
- **Trigger:** live content; the viewer stays paused inside a pause window longer than the time-shift buffer, so the suspended position is gone on resume.
- **Why it matters:** PLY-59 L1258-1261 (*"A mechanism that cannot restore that position is not a pause under this specification"*) cannot be met; §8.8 L3660-3670 resumes at the oldest available media, as the base does for its own resumption (Table 62 NOTE 1, *"If RT is in the past, the playback shall start from the oldest available media segment (the edge of the timeshift buffer)"*), and leaves open whether that is still a pause (§8.13 item 4, L3732). Annex H.8 L5588-5596 walks exactly this case.
- **Responsible actor:** Player. `context/` is silent on buffer expiry (R37.2 addresses mechanism, R25.1 a Player-chosen jump to live).
- **Routing:** 5.c (D-3).

## Ambiguities (12)

### A-1 — R1.4 ends a failed ad at the primary content; DP-3 requires the fallback chain first
- **Context passages:** R1.4, 03:164-170: *"the Player MUST abort that ad and continue playing the primary content uninterrupted"*. DP-3, 03:68-73: *"The first consequence: **an opportunity is given up only after every way of filling it that the manifest declares has been tried.**"*, and the next overlapping window or the declared fallback *"is attempted (R20.1, R40.3) before the Player continues with the primary content."*
- **Readings:** (a) R1.4 governs a runtime failure on its own terms: the Player aborts and returns to the primary content, even when nothing of the attempt was rendered and a fallback window exists; (b) an attempt whose runtime failures leave nothing rendered produced no ad, and R20.1 governs, as R5.3 says for candidates that cannot render at all.
- **Spec assumed:** (b). PLY-86 L1417-1422: *"When the attempt ends with no candidate rendered, it produced no ad and PLY-20 governs what follows; otherwise the Player MUST continue playing the primary content uninterrupted."*; E11 L3592, precedence step 4 L3606-3607, R.6 E11 L7421.
- **Resolution chain:** DP-3 picks (b), and so does the base for the linear family: §5.16.2.2.6 lists among the failure conditions *"The playback of the alternative presentation cannot start"*, with the reason *"Media playback is impossible due to missing media or initialization segments"*, and on a failed execution §5.16.2.2.5 step 2 d continues: *"If execution fails, steps a-c above are repeated for next events in QE"*. R1.4's own sentence says it *"matches what the base specification already requires"* (03:167-168).
- **Routing:** 5.d (P-1).

### A-2 — Whether drop-before-play on a List MPD is a departure from the base (audit K-34)
- **Context passage:** 03:782-784 R7.3 (*"The Player MAY drop a candidate before playback ("drop before play") when its declared duration would push the cumulative slot duration past the cap"*) and R1.5 03:171-178.

- **Spec assumed:** neither; L1099-1104 and §8.13 item 11 L3770-3773 leave it open.
- **Routing:** 5.b (F-2).

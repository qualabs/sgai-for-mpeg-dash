# DASH conformance audit — v12.4 (2026-09-26)

**Audit target**: `../output/v12.4-sgai-spec.md`.
**Reference**: the edition declared in
`../context/00-normative-base.md` (ISO/IEC 23009-1:2026, sixth edition).
**Method**: inventory + clause searches against the primary copy +
per-construct verdict.

The key words MUST, SHOULD and MAY in suggested fixes are used as in
IETF RFC 2119.

## Scope

This audit covers every construct v12.4 introduces or modifies in a DASH
document, and the inherited base constructs whose semantics v12.4 restates
or constrains: the two window schemes and their child elements and
attributes, the List MPD additions, the empty linear resolution, the
sub-MPDs, both tracking carriers, the standalone `<svta:OverlayList>` and its
schema, the request-parameter rules, the profile declarations, the
pause-delivery `Metrics` request, and the Player rules that restate the base
Alternative-MPD processing model. Design quality is out of scope.

v12.4 is a minor refine of v12.3 and changes prose only. For this audit the
relevant edits are three. The §4.8.3 bullet that grounded PLY-41 on a closed
reading of DASH §5.16.2.2.6 is removed, as is the same reasoning in Annex
L.7. A new §8.13 item 14 records PLY-41 against the open list of reasons of
§5.16.2.2.6. A scripted creative is now carried as `text/html` (§3.5,
§5.2.2). The other edits (PLY-34, PLY-47, the pause slot of PLY-32, the cap
wording in §7 and Annex E.7, the refinement-gap table) touch no DASH
construct. No DASH construct is added or removed. The item IDs and their
constructs are those of the v12.3 audit, K-1 to K-36.

## Method summary

- **Inventory.** Carried from the v12.3 audit and re-checked against a full
  `diff` of v12.3 and v12.4 (123 lines), read line by line. The §8.13 item
  numbers the verdicts cite (1, 9, 10, 11, 12) are unchanged; item 14 is new.
- **Primary copy.** The PDF whose SHA-256 equals `primary_copy.sha256`
  (`a7274eb2…be100`), checked before extraction; `bin/check-normative-base.py`
  exited 0. Extracted once with `pdftotext -layout` into a private RAM
  directory. The licence header and footer lines were stripped before any
  search, and a grep for them afterwards returned 0 (the same grep on the
  unstripped extraction returned 1152). Whitespace was flattened so that
  phrases spanning line breaks match.
- **Quotations.** A script extracted the 99 distinct italic quotations of
  v12.4 (one new against v12.3, none removed), split them at ellipses, and
  looked each fragment up in the flattened text with quotes and dashes
  normalised. A known-present phrase matched and an invented one did not.
  97 matched verbatim. The two others are the same as in v12.3: *"a key use
  case"* differs only in the capital of its first word (§5.16.1), and the
  `@skipAfter` sentence of Table 63 is split in the extraction by the
  table's *Default* column. The new quotation, *"include (but are not limited
  to)"*, spans a line break and is found in §5.16.2.2.6, where the spec cites
  it.
- **Clause attribution.** The three sentences K-36 rests on were placed under
  their headings in the stripped extraction: *"If execution fails, steps a-c
  above are repeated for next events in QE"* under §5.16.2.2.5, *"Execution
  fails if at least one of the conditions below is true at PRTA"* and the
  open list of reasons under §5.16.2.2.6.
- **Structural checks.** The 83 `xml` blocks of v12.4 are byte-identical to
  those of v12.3, compared block by block, so the structural results recorded
  for them still hold. All 83 re-parse as well-formed; a malformed control
  document was rejected.
- Each construct is assessed Conforming / Marginal / Non-conforming.

## Base-standard findings (highlights)

- **Alternative MPD execution.** *"Execution fails if at least one of the
  conditions below is true at PRTA"*. One condition is *"The playback of the
  alternative presentation cannot start"*, and *"The reasons for this include
  (but are not limited to) the following"* (§5.16.2.2.6). On failure, *"steps
  a-c above are repeated for next events in QE"* (§5.16.2.2.5 step 2 d). E.c
  counts the times *"alternative MPD playback successfully started"*
  (§5.16.2.2.2).
- **Merge.** §5.3.2.6.3 considers the device in one place only: an MPD-level
  `EssentialProperty` that *"is not supported"* makes the resolution fail
  (step 3 a ii). No step removes a merged Period for being undecodable.
- **Extension points.** `EventType`, `PeriodType` and `EventStreamType`
  admit `xs:any ##other`; `EventStreamType` admits no foreign attribute;
  `ImportedMpdType` admits `anyAttribute` only.
- **Profiles.** The List profile *"is an extension of the ISO-BMFF CMAF
  Profile"* (§8.14). Profile conformance needs *"at least one Representation
  in each Period"* (§8.1). §8.13.2.2 names Alternative MPD and Callback
  schemes for Advanced Linear `EventStream`s.

## Non-conforming items detail

### K-36 — PLY-41 turns a base failed execution into a non-failure on the linear family

**Rule.** §5.16.2.2.6: *"Execution fails if at least one of the conditions
below is true at PRTA: … The playback of the alternative presentation cannot
start. The reasons for this include (but are not limited to) the following
…"*. §5.16.2.2.5 step 2 d: *"If execution fails, steps a-c above are
repeated for next events in QE"*. §5.16.2.2.1 presents both as *"a set of
normative conditions which hold for any sequence of Alternative MPD
events"*.

**Conflict.** PLY-41 still says "A resolution document carrying candidates
is **not** a failed execution, whatever the Player then does with them", and
it is still not scoped to the non-linear families. For a List MPD whose
candidates the device cannot play, playback of the alternative presentation
cannot start, so the base fails the execution and tries the next queued
Alternative MPD event. A Player conformant to v12.4 stays on the primary
content instead (PLY-41; §6.5, the `200` row; §7.12 path 4; §8.2 row E7,
"not the next window"). DOC-3 still says the one place where this
specification's Player does not execute a base event the base would execute
is PLY-48 in §4.8.3; the next queued linear event skipped here is a second
one.

**What changed since v12.3.** The v12.3 fix had two parts. The second is
done: §4.8.3 no longer grounds the rule on a closed reading of
§5.16.2.2.6, Annex L.7 no longer does either, and §8.13 item 14 quotes the
condition and its open list correctly. The first is not: the rule is neither
scoped nor declared as a departure. Recording a conflict in §8.13 makes it
visible; it does not make the behaviour conform. Annex L.7 itself is an
overlay (non-linear) case and is not in conflict.

**Where the fix lives.** In `context/` R20.1, which states that "a
resolution document carrying candidates is not a failed execution" and also
adopts the linear family unchanged from the base (refinement gap 17,
validation P-5, routed as a predictable `context/` change).

**Suggested fix.** In `context/` R20.1, either:

- scope the non-failure sentence to the non-linear families, and state that
  on the linear family a List MPD with no candidate the device can start is a
  failed execution under §5.16.2.2.6; or
- declare it a departure with scope and reason, in which case §4.8.3 MUST
  list it and DOC-3 MUST stop saying there is one place.

Either way, §8.13 item 14 then goes away or becomes the departure's pointer.

## Marginal items detail

### K-17 — Empty linear resolution vs the List profile's CMAF base

§8.13 item 9 names *"The playback of the alternative presentation cannot
start"* as the condition the empty shape falls under, with the merge
condition as its closest analogue; §5.2.3 and PLY-39 say the same. The
document is schema-valid, and Table 4 admits it (*"At least one Adaptation
Set shall be present in each Period unless the value of the @duration
attribute of the Period is set to zero."*). But the List profile *"is an
extension of the ISO-BMFF CMAF Profile"* (§8.14), and a Media Presentation
conforms to a profile only if *"There is at least one Representation in each
Period in the profile-specific MPD"* (§8.1). §8.13 item 9 records this, so
it is an open question and not a conflict.

**Suggested clarification.** None beyond §8.13 item 9 (refinement gap 4,
validation F-4).

### K-29 — SGAI event streams under Advanced Linear

§8.13.1 announces *"Support for a restricted set of DASH events"*.
§8.13.2.2 says *"EventStream elements may indicate Alternative MPD (5.16)
and Callback (5.10.4.5) event schemes"*. §8.13.2.1 says *"Periods and
Representations which do not conform to the constraints in this subclause
may not be presented."* The base's own K.6.4 example carries a scheme
§8.13.2.2 does not name, which suggests the list is not exhaustive; that
does not settle it for application schemes.

**Suggested clarification.** Keep it open and route it to the base editors
(§8.13 item 1; refinement gap 5, validation F-5).

### K-34 — Drop-before-play of a List MPD Period (PLY-35)

For insertion the base trims: *"For insertion events, APDA = min(APD,
APDmax)"* (Table 57). It has no rule that drops a Period of a successfully
merged List MPD because of its declared duration, and PLY-35 permits that
(MAY). This is Marginal and not Non-conforming because the base states no
explicit obligation to play every Period: step 2 of the merge already
removes invalid Periods. §4.5.5 and §8.13 item 11 acknowledge it without
recording it in §4.8.3.

**Suggested clarification.** Either restrict PLY-35 (R7.3) to the non-linear
families, or record it in §4.8.3 as a departure scoped to List MPDs
(refinement gap 2, validation F-2).

### K-35 — `urn:svta:` is not a registered URN namespace

Table 43 asks only that *"The string may use URN or URL syntax"*. A URN with
an unregistered NID has URN syntax but is not a formal URN. No base rule is
violated, and §8.13 item 12 records it.

**Suggested clarification.** None beyond §8.13 item 12 (refinement gap 6,
validation F-6).

## Open questions surfaced

- **`StringVectorType` as an XML Schema list** (§5.0, §4.8.2). The type
  definition is not in the PDF body: a search for
  `simpleType name="StringVectorType"` returns 0, while `simpleType name="` alone
  finds 9 other base types. The type sits in the separately published
  `DASH-MPD.xsd`. The prose (*"whitespace-separated list"* for
  `@dependencyId`) supports the claim, and it bears on no verdict. Every
  other clause lookup of this audit located its source.

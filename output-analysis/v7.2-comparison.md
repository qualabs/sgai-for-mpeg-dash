## Verdict

**REGRESSION**

Total issues rose from 41 to 45 (Δ = +4). The contract's threshold for
REGRESSION is a net increase of +1 or more, and the arithmetic clears it.

The headline number is honest and is also, on the evidence of the six
sidecars, almost entirely an artefact of how the two iterations were
measured rather than of what changed in the spec: **+5 of the +4 net is
the same finding being counted in two places at once**, and the
refinement demonstrably closed seven findings that v7.1 raised. The
breakdown is in `## Metric caveats` below, which is where the decision
between another minor (`v7.3`), a major (`v8`) and a rollback should be
taken from — that decision belongs to the orchestrator, not to this
document.

### What the sidecars cannot settle

For the six genuinely new findings (A-2, A-7 and detail flags 2–5) the
sidecars give **no basis to distinguish "introduced by the refinement"
from "found by a fresh validator on unchanged text"**. None of the six
cites a pre-v7.2 gap-table row, and none is located in a section the
v7.2 detail review attributes to a refine edit. The precedent cuts both
ways: v7.1's own autofix 1 repaired a line-wrap that the v7.1
refinement had broken, so refinements in this pipeline do sometimes
introduce defects. Resolving this requires reading the spec's
`<!-- refine: … -->` markers against the finding sites, which this step
is contractually forbidden from doing.

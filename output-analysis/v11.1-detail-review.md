## Open questions surfaced

- **Six more `refine:` comments split a paragraph inside a list item.** The
  six are at L1568, L1714, L1811, L3513, L3521 and L3537. They are indented,
  so no list breaks. But each is a block-level element, so the text around
  it renders as two paragraphs. At L1568 and L3521 an original paragraph is
  split. Nothing is lost, and these were left as they are. The cause for all
  eight sites is the same: `refine-spec.prompt` says to place the comment "at
  the nearest line above the edited content". At column 0 that ends a list,
  and inside a paragraph it ends the paragraph. The fix belongs in that
  prompt, for example "inline at the end of the line, or at the item's
  content column after its paragraph". The split scan used here (block
  `html_block` tokens between two list or paragraph tokens) is small enough
  to become a check under `bin/`.
- **`## Refinement gaps` (L7181) is a heading outside the table of
  contents.** `refine-spec.prompt` requires the section, and it is correct in
  a candidate. At promotion into `dist/` it has to go, and nothing in the
  pipeline removes it. The same holds for the build number in the status
  line (L3).
- **Five Type cells do not name the schema type.** They read `enum` or "list
  of `xs:anyURI`, space-separated": L1992, L2020, L2118, L2121 and L2471.
  §5.10.1 declares `LinearRelationType`, `OnTopOnlyType`, `FamilyType`,
  `OnExhaustedType` and `URIListType` for them. §5.0 now presents the types
  it defines "under the names the schema of §5.10.1 declares". A reader
  checking these five cells against the schema finds no name to match, which
  is the same kind of problem as v11 flag 1. This one is milder, because the
  cells describe the value space correctly.

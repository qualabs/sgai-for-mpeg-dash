# DASH conformance audit — v12.2 (2026-09-26)

**Audit target**: `../output/v12.2-sgai-spec.md` (with the Step 7.5 autofix
already applied).
**Reference**: the edition declared in
`../context/00-normative-base.md` (ISO/IEC 23009-1:2026, sixth edition).
**Method**: inventory + clause searches against the primary copy +
per-construct verdict.

The key words MUST, SHOULD and MAY in suggested fixes are used as in
IETF RFC 2119.

## Scope

This audit covers every construct v12.2 introduces or modifies in a DASH
document, and the inherited base constructs whose semantics v12.2 restates
or constrains. That means the two window schemes and their child elements
and attributes, the List MPD additions, the empty linear resolution, the
sub-MPDs, both tracking carriers, the standalone `<svta:OverlayList>` and
its schema, the request-parameter rules, the profile declarations, the
pause-delivery `Metrics` request, and the Player rules that restate the base
Alternative-MPD processing model. Design quality is out of scope.

v12.2 differs from v12 in the refine edits of v12.1 (K-12, K-17, K-19,
K-21, K-27 of the v12 audit, and the schema-type listing in §5.0) and in
the context delta of v12.2 (APS-1 and the §1.2 APS row, PLY-1). The v12.2
delta adds no DASH construct. Every construct was nevertheless re-checked
against the primary copy, and the item IDs of the v12 audit are kept so the
`<!-- refine: …#K-nn -->` annotations in the spec still resolve.

## Method summary

- The inventory was built from §1.4, §2.1, §4.2–§4.5, §4.7, §4.8, all of
  chapter 5 (including the §5.10.1 schema) and §8.13, and from all 83 `xml`
  blocks of the spec (Annexes A–R included).
- **Primary copy.** It is the PDF whose SHA-256 equals
  `primary_copy.sha256` (`a7274eb2…be100`), checked before extraction;
  `bin/check-normative-base.py` also exited 0. The PDF was extracted once
  with `pdftotext -layout`. The licence header and footer lines were
  stripped before any search; a grep for them afterwards returned 0.
  Whitespace was flattened so phrases spanning line breaks match.
- **Quotations.** A script checked all 98 distinct italic quotations in the
  spec against the flattened text, with quotes and dashes normalised and
  split at ellipses. 97 matched verbatim. The 98th (the `@skipAfter`
  sentence of Table 63) is split in the extraction by the table's *Default*
  column (*"… till "PT0S" the moment …"*). Read in place, it is quoted
  correctly.
- **Clause attribution.** A second pass located 70 quoted phrases in the
  clean text and compared the governing clause and table with the ones the
  spec cites. Every one matched: for example *"The playback of the
  alternative presentation cannot start"* is in §5.16.2.2.6, *"The dispatch
  mode of this event scheme is "on-receive""* is in §5.16.3 and §5.16.4,
  *"The information in the @profiles parameters is merged"* is in
  §5.3.2.6.3 step 3 a i, and *"This extended scheme is signalled through the
  use of EssentialProperty or SupplementalProperty"* is in I.3.1.
- **Direct reads.** About 40 targeted phrase and schema searches. The
  schema fragments read were `MPDtype`, `PeriodType`, `EventStreamType`,
  `EventType`, `AlternativeMPDEventType`, `AlternativeMPDReplaceEventType`,
  `ImportedMpdType`, `ServiceDescriptionType` and
  `PlaybackRestrictionsType`. The clauses read were Tables 43, 44 and 47,
  §5.2.3.2, §5.3.2.6.3, §5.10.2.4, §5.16.2.2.6, §8.13.1–§8.13.2.7, §8.14,
  §8.15.2, I.3.1 and A.13.7. Every search that returned a hit is a positive
  control for the method. The zero-hit searches, each with its positive
  control, are in *Open questions*.
- **Structural checker.** A checker was run over all 83 XML blocks. It
  checks:
  - well-formedness;
  - the child order of `MPD`, `Period`, `EventStream` and `<svta:Ad>`
    against the sixth-edition sequences;
  - foreign attributes on `EventStream`, and `@value` on SGAI streams;
  - non-decreasing `@presentationTime` and duplicate `Event@id`;
  - Insertion events in dynamic MPDs, `@status` on Insertion events, and
    `@clip` without `@maxDuration`;
  - windows without `@durationCap`, `Event@duration` or `Event@id`, and
    window tokens outside their family;
  - `@customRegion` without `custom`, and `@linearRelation` values;
  - List MPD profile and `ImportedMPD` presence;
  - the §8.15.2/§8.15.3 constraints on every SPS sub-MPD;
  - the chapter-5 co-occurrence rules on `<svta:RenderableAsset>` and
    `<svta:OverlayList>`, and the scheme and `@value` of `<svta:Tracking>`.

  Before it was trusted, it was run on three synthetic documents carrying
  seeded violations, and it reported every seeded class. On the spec it
  reported one item, the expected one: the empty List MPD of §5.2.3, a
  Period without `ImportedMPD`, by design.
- Each construct is assessed Conforming / Marginal / Non-conforming.

## Base-standard findings (highlights)

- **Extension points.** `MPD`, `Period`, `Event`, `EventStream` and
  `AlternativeMPDEventType` admit `xs:any ##other`. `EventStreamType` admits
  no foreign attribute. `ImportedMpdType` is simple content with
  `anyAttribute` only, and its `earliestResolutionTimeOffset` is `xs:double`,
  default 60.0. DR-2 matches the schema text.
- **Events.** Table 44 carries the three semantics §5.5.2 now re-anchors:
  the Period-relative timebase, *"Each EventStream element shall not contain
  two Event elements with the same value of Event@id"*, and the
  presentation-wide `@id` scope. §5.10.2.4 ignores an already-processed event
  whose `@status` is absent. Both base Alternative MPD schemes declare
  *"on-receive"*; A.13.7 (informative) gives the on-receive reading of
  `@duration`.
- **Alternative MPD execution.** *"Execution fails if at least one of the
  conditions below is true at PRTA"*, and one of them is *"The playback of
  the alternative presentation cannot start"*, whose reasons include
  *"Media playback is impossible due to missing media or initialization
  segments"* (§5.16.2.2.6). This is still the rule PLY-41 conflicts with.
- **Profiles.** §8.13.1 announces *"Alternative MPD and Callback MPD events
  for interoperable server-guided ad insertion"* under *"Support for a
  restricted set of DASH events"*. §8.13.2.4 permits `RequestParam` with
  `altmpd` in an `EventStream` carrying Alternative MPD events. §8.15.2
  excludes MPD-level `Metrics` and `SupplementalProperty` from sub-MPDs.

## Non-conforming items detail

### K-36 — PLY-41 turns a base failed execution into a non-failure on the linear family

**Rule.** §5.16.2.2.6: *"Execution fails if at least one of the conditions
below is true at PRTA: … The playback of the alternative presentation cannot
start. The reasons for this include (but are not limited to) the following
… Media playback is impossible due to missing media or initialization
segments."* §5.16.2.2.5 step 2 d: *"If execution fails, steps a-c above are
repeated for next events in QE"*. §5.16.2.2.1 presents both as *"a set of
normative conditions which hold for any sequence of Alternative MPD
events"*.

**Conflict.** PLY-41 (unchanged in v12.2) says "A resolution document
carrying candidates is **not** a failed execution, whatever the Player then
does with them", and it is not scoped to the non-linear families. Take a
List MPD whose candidates the device cannot play. The base says playback
cannot start, so the execution fails and the next queued Alternative MPD
event is tried. A Player conformant to v12.2 stops at the primary content
instead (§7.12 path 4 applies the same rule).

That is a Player of this specification not executing a base event the base
would execute. §4.8.3 lists exactly one such case, PLY-48, and DOC-3 says
that PLY-48 is "The one place". §8.13 item 12 and refinement gap 1
acknowledge that the departure is not recorded.

**Suggested fix.** Scope PLY-41 to `<svta:OverlayList>` documents. For a
List MPD, state that a Player unable to start any candidate treats the
execution as failed under §5.16.2.2.6 and continues down the chain
(PLY-38). If the project keeps the behaviour on the linear family, it MUST
list it in §4.8.3 with scope and reason, and DOC-3 MUST stop calling PLY-48
the only departure. This is the R1.5 question the spec routes to `context/`
(refinement gap 1, validation D-7).

## Marginal items detail

### K-17 — Empty linear resolution vs the List profile's CMAF base

**Resolved since v12.** §5.2.3 and PLY-39 now name *"The playback of the
alternative presentation cannot start"* as the governing condition. They
keep *"Alternative MPD is a List MPD, and merge process resulted in no
available media"* only as the closest analogue, because with no
`ImportedMPD` no merge takes place.

**Still open.** The document is schema-valid, and Table 4 admits it (*"At
least one Adaptation Set shall be present in each Period unless the value of
the @duration attribute of the Period is set to zero."*). But the List
profile *"is an extension of the ISO-BMFF CMAF Profile"* (§8.14), and a
Media Presentation conforms to a profile only if *"There is at least one
Representation in each Period in the profile-specific MPD"* (§8.1). §8.13
item 10 records this. The item still says the base "anticipates 'merge
process resulted in no available media'" for this shape and calls it "the
base's own failure condition". That is the reading §5.2.3 has just set
aside. This is a v12.2 detail-review flag, and it affects an informative
chapter only.

**Suggested clarification.** In §8.13 item 10, cite *"The playback of the
alternative presentation cannot start"* as the condition the empty shape
falls under, as §5.2.3 does. Keep the profile-conformance question open.

### K-29 — SGAI event streams under Advanced Linear

**Ambiguity.** §8.13.1 announces *"Support for a restricted set of DASH
events"*, listing the MPD Validity Expiration, Alternative MPD and Callback
events. §8.13.2.2 says *"EventStream elements may indicate Alternative MPD
(5.16) and Callback (5.10.4.5) event schemes"*, and §8.13.2.1 says *"Periods
and Representations which do not conform to the constraints in this
subclause may not be presented."* The base's own Advanced Linear example
(K.6.4) carries an `EventStream` of
`urn:mpeg:dash:event:service-description:2024`, a scheme §8.13.2.2 does not
name. That supports reading the list as non-exhaustive, but does not settle
the question for application schemes. PUB-18 routes `RequestParam`
Publishers here, and now says so.

**Suggested clarification.** Keep it open and route it to the base editors,
as §8.13 item 1 proposes.

### K-34 — Drop-before-play of a List MPD Period (PLY-35)

**Ambiguity.** For insertion the base bounds the alternative presentation by
trimming: *"For insertion events, APDA = min(APD, APDmax)"* (Table 57). It
has no rule that drops a Period of a successfully merged List MPD because of
its declared duration, and PLY-35 permits exactly that (MAY). This is
Marginal and not Non-conforming because the base states no explicit
obligation to play every Period: a Period can already be removed during the
merge (step 2). But what plays then differs from the base model, and §4.5.5
and §8.13 item 12 acknowledge this without recording it in §4.8.3.

**Suggested clarification.** Either restrict PLY-35 to the non-linear
families, or record it in §4.8.3 as a declared departure scoped to List MPDs
(refinement gap 2).

### K-35 — `urn:svta:` is not a registered URN namespace

**Ambiguity.** Table 43 asks only that *"The string may use URN or URL
syntax"*, and recommends a month-date for URLs. A URN with an unregistered
NID has URN syntax but is not a formal URN. Nothing in the base is
violated, and the spec records the point as §8.13 item 13.

**Suggested clarification.** None beyond §8.13 item 13. Registering the NID,
or moving to a dated URL under an SVTA-owned domain, would remove it.

## Open questions surfaced

- **`StringVectorType` as an XML Schema list** (§5.0, §4.8.2, cited as the
  model for `LayoutTokenListType`). A search for `StringVectorType` in the
  extracted text returned 6 hits, all attribute declarations. A search for
  `simpleType name="StringVectorType"` returned 0. The positive control, the
  same pattern for other base types, found `RatioType`, `FrameRateType`,
  `SwitchingTypeType` and others. The type definition therefore sits in the
  separately published `DASH-MPD.xsd`, not in the PDF body. The prose found

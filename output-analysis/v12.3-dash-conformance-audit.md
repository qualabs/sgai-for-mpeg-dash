# DASH conformance audit — v12.3 (2026-09-26)

**Audit target**: `../output/v12.3-sgai-spec.md`.
**Reference**: the edition declared in
`../context/00-normative-base.md` (ISO/IEC 23009-1:2026, sixth edition).
**Method**: inventory + clause searches against the primary copy +
per-construct verdict.

The key words MUST, SHOULD and MAY in suggested fixes are used as in
IETF RFC 2119.

## Scope

This audit covers every construct v12.3 introduces or modifies in a DASH
document, and the inherited base constructs whose semantics v12.3 restates
or constrains. That means the two window schemes and their child elements
and attributes, the List MPD additions, the empty linear resolution, the
sub-MPDs, both tracking carriers, the standalone `<svta:OverlayList>` and
its schema, the request-parameter rules, the profile declarations, the
pause-delivery `Metrics` request, and the Player rules that restate the base
Alternative-MPD processing model. Design quality is out of scope.

v12.3 is a minor refine of v12.2. It changes prose only: what the pause
window's `@durationCap` bounds (validation T1), a reading rule for the paused
frame (T2), PLY-41 moved from §8.13 into the §4.8.3 list of non-departures
(T3), a sentence on `<svta:Tracking>` as the one candidate-level carrier
(T4), the double-box background surface (T5), and §8.13 item 9 now citing the
base condition K-17 asked for. §8.13 is renumbered: the old items 10–14 are
now 9–13. No DASH construct is added or removed. Every construct was
nevertheless re-checked against the primary copy. The item IDs are those of
the v12.2 audit, written in the `K-<n>` form without zero padding, so that
K-17, K-34 and K-36 name the same constructs as before.

## Method summary

- The inventory was built from §1.4, §2.1, §4.2–§4.5, §4.7, §4.8, all of
  chapter 5 (including the §5.10.1 schema) and §8.13, and from all 83 `xml`
  blocks of the spec (Annexes A–R included). A `diff` against v12.2 was read
  in full (169 lines) to find every changed sentence.
- **Primary copy.** It is the PDF whose SHA-256 equals
  `primary_copy.sha256` (`a7274eb2…be100`), checked before extraction;
  `bin/check-normative-base.py` also exited 0. The PDF was extracted once
  with `pdftotext -layout` into a private RAM directory. The licence header
  and footer lines were stripped before any search, and a grep for them
  afterwards returned 0. Whitespace was flattened so that phrases spanning
  line breaks match.
- **Quotations.** A script checked all 106 distinct italic quotations in the
  spec against the flattened text, with quotes and dashes normalised and
  split at ellipses. A known-present phrase matched and an invented one did
  not. 104 matched verbatim. One (*"a key use case"*, §1.4) differs only in
  the capital of its first word, which the base writes at the start of a
  sentence (*"A key use case for this is advertisement"*, §5.16.1). One (the
  `@skipAfter` sentence of Table 63) is split in the extraction by the
  table's *Default* column (*"… till "PT0S" the moment …"*); read in place it
  is quoted correctly.
- **Clause attribution.** 20 load-bearing phrases were located in the clean
  text and their governing clause and table compared with the ones the spec
  cites. All matched. Three results of the automatic heading lookup were
  wrong and were checked by hand: the CMAF sentence is in §8.14, the
  *"may be ignored"* sentence occurs in §8.2.2–§8.7.2 and so also in §8.4.2
  (the one the spec cites), and the *"at least one Representation"* sentence
  is in §8.1.
- **Direct reads.** The schema fragments read were `EventStreamType` (no
  `xs:anyAttribute` in its range, against three hits elsewhere as the
  positive control), `EventType` and `ImportedMpdType`. The clauses read were
  §5.3.2.6.3 (all steps), §5.16.2.2.6 (all conditions and NOTE 3), the
  §5.2.3.2 list (it contains `Period.EventStream`) and the §8.4.2 sentence.
- **Structural checks.** The 83 `xml` blocks of v12.3 are byte-identical to
  those of v12.2, compared block by block. The structural results the v12.2
  audit recorded for them (child order, foreign attributes on
  `EventStream`, event constraints, sub-MPD constraints, co-occurrence rules)
  therefore still hold; that checker was not re-run. All 83 blocks were
  re-parsed and are well-formed; a malformed control document was rejected.
- Each construct is assessed Conforming / Marginal / Non-conforming.

## Base-standard findings (highlights)

- **Alternative MPD execution.** *"Execution fails if at least one of the
  conditions below is true at PRTA"*. One condition is *"The playback of the
  alternative presentation cannot start"*, and *"The reasons for this include
  (but are not limited to) the following"*: an unavailable or invalid MPD, a
  merge with no available media, and missing media or initialization segments
  (§5.16.2.2.6). The list is open, and the condition is that playback cannot
  start. E.c counts the times *"alternative MPD playback successfully
  started"* (§5.16.2.2.2).
- **Merge.** §5.3.2.6.3 does consider the device in one place: an MPD-level
  `EssentialProperty` that *"is not supported"* makes the resolution fail
  (step 3 a ii). No step removes a merged Period for being undecodable.
- **Extension points.** `EventType`, `PeriodType` and `EventStreamType`
  admit `xs:any ##other`; `EventStreamType` admits no foreign attribute;
  `ImportedMpdType` admits `anyAttribute` only.
- **Profiles.** The List profile *"is an extension of the ISO-BMFF CMAF
  Profile"* (§8.14). Profile conformance needs *"at least one Representation
  in each Period"* (§8.1). §8.13.2.2 names Alternative MPD and Callback
  schemes for Advanced Linear `EventStream`s.

## Non-conforming items detail

### K-36 — PLY-41 turns a base failed execution into a non-failure on the linear family

**Rule.** §5.16.2.2.6: *"Execution fails if at least one of the conditions
below is true at PRTA: … The playback of the alternative presentation cannot
start. The reasons for this include (but are not limited to) the following
…"*. §5.16.2.2.5 step 2 d: *"If execution fails, steps a-c above are
repeated for next events in QE"*. §5.16.2.2.1 presents both as *"a set of
normative conditions which hold for any sequence of Alternative MPD
events"*.

**Conflict.** PLY-41 still says "A resolution document carrying candidates
is **not** a failed execution, whatever the Player then does with them", and
it is not scoped to the non-linear families. Take a List MPD whose candidates
the device cannot play. Under the base, playback of the alternative
presentation cannot start, so the execution fails and the next queued
Alternative MPD event is tried. A Player conformant to v12.3 stops at the
primary content instead (PLY-41; §7.12 path 4; §6.5, the `200` row; §8.2 row E7, "not
the next window").

v12.3 now lists this in §4.8.3 among the rules that "look like departures and
are not". Its reason is that "The base condition is media availability after
the merge (DASH §5.16.2.2.6), not renderability on a device". The clause does
not say that. The condition is *"The playback of the alternative presentation
cannot start"*. Media availability after the merge is one of its listed
reasons, and the list is declared open (*"include (but are not limited
to)"*). Nothing in §5.16.2.2.6 limits the condition to the listed reasons.
E.c, which counts playbacks that *"successfully started"*, is not incremented
either. The v12.2 audit had this as §8.13 item 12. v12.3 dropped it from
§8.13, so the conflict is no longer acknowledged anywhere in the normative
chapters. Refinement gap 12 is the only place that still records it.

**Where the fix lives.** The sentence comes from `context/`: R20.1 states "A
resolution document that carries candidates is not a failed execution", and also says the linear family is "adopted unchanged" from the base. For a
List MPD the device cannot play, those two statements contradict each other,
so the spec cannot resolve this alone (refinement gap 12 says the same).

**Suggested fix.** In `context/` R20.1, either:

- scope the non-failure sentence to the non-linear families, and state that
  on the linear family a List MPD with no candidate the device can start is a
  failed execution under §5.16.2.2.6; or
- declare it a departure with scope and reason, in which case §4.8.3 MUST
  list it and DOC-3 MUST stop saying there is one place.

Whichever is chosen, the §4.8.3 bullet added by T3 MUST NOT ground the rule
on a closed reading of §5.16.2.2.6.

## Marginal items detail

### K-17 — Empty linear resolution vs the List profile's CMAF base

**Resolved since v12.2.** §8.13 item 9 now names *"The playback of the
alternative presentation cannot start"* as the condition the empty shape
falls under, with the merge condition as its closest analogue. §5.2.3 and
PLY-39 say the same.

**Still open.** The document is schema-valid, and Table 4 admits it (*"At
least one Adaptation Set shall be present in each Period unless the value of
the @duration attribute of the Period is set to zero."*). But the List
profile *"is an extension of the ISO-BMFF CMAF Profile"* (§8.14), and a Media
Presentation conforms to a profile only if *"There is at least one
Representation in each Period in the profile-specific MPD"* (§8.1). §8.13
item 9 records this, so it is an open question and not a conflict.

**Suggested clarification.** None beyond §8.13 item 9.

### K-29 — SGAI event streams under Advanced Linear

**Ambiguity.** §8.13.1 announces *"Support for a restricted set of DASH
events"*. §8.13.2.2 says *"EventStream elements may indicate Alternative MPD
(5.16) and Callback (5.10.4.5) event schemes"*. §8.13.2.1 says *"Periods and
Representations which do not conform to the constraints in this subclause
may not be presented."* The base's own K.6.4 example carries a scheme
§8.13.2.2 does not name, which suggests the list is not exhaustive. That
does not settle the question for application schemes.

**Suggested clarification.** Keep it open and route it to the base editors
(§8.13 item 1).

### K-34 — Drop-before-play of a List MPD Period (PLY-35)

**Ambiguity.** For insertion the base trims: *"For insertion events, APDA =
min(APD, APDmax)"* (Table 57). It has no rule that drops a Period of a
successfully merged List MPD because of its declared duration, and PLY-35
permits that (MAY). This is Marginal and not Non-conforming because the base
states no explicit obligation to play every Period: step 2 of the merge
already removes invalid Periods. §4.5.5 and §8.13 item 11 acknowledge it
without recording it in §4.8.3.

**Suggested clarification.** Either restrict PLY-35 (R7.3) to the non-linear
families, or record it in §4.8.3 as a departure scoped to List MPDs
(refinement gap 13, validation F-2).

### K-35 — `urn:svta:` is not a registered URN namespace

**Ambiguity.** Table 43 asks only that *"The string may use URN or URL
syntax"*. A URN with an unregistered NID has URN syntax but is not a formal
URN. No base rule is violated, and §8.13 item 12 records it.

**Suggested clarification.** None beyond §8.13 item 12.

## Open questions surfaced

- **`StringVectorType` as an XML Schema list** (§5.0, §4.8.2). As in v12.2,
  the type definition is not in the PDF body: a search for
  `simpleType name="StringVectorType"` returned 0, and the same pattern found
  other base types. The type sits in the separately published `DASH-MPD.xsd`.
  The prose (*"whitespace-separated list"* for `@dependencyId`) supports the
  claim, and it bears on no verdict. Every other clause lookup of this audit
  located its source.

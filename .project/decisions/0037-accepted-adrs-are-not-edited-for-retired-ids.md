# ADR 0037: Accepted ADRs are not edited for retired ids

This document uses RFC 2119 vocabulary (MUST / SHOULD / MAY).

## Context

19 mentions of retiring ids sit in seven accepted ADRs (0011, 0014, 0018,
0022, 0023, 0024 and 0028). Accepted ADRs are a record.

## Decision

They are not edited and carry no note: the "Retired identifiers" table
(ADR 0036) resolves those citations.

## Consequences

- A reader of an old ADR follows a retired id through the table.
